How to Use

1. Add your medicine.
2. Set the reminder time.
3. Save the medicine reminder.
4. Scan the QR code to view medicine details.
💡

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How can I add a medicine?
A: Go to Add Medicine and enter the medicine details.
Q: How can I view my medicines?
A: Open My Medicines from the dashboard.
Q: How can I scan a QR code?
A: Select Scan QR Code and scan the medicine QR code.
